---

### 🌀 **Wild (Pericentral)**

This is raw motor activity, sensorimotor immediacy—the chaotic, embodied swirl before meaning. *Pericentral* is primal: it initiates contact with the world not through thought, but gesture. Wild is not untamed—it is pre-symbolic. Not dangerous, but necessary. The glyph 🌀 is apt because it's both vortex and spiral staircase: you're caught in motion, but there's potential ascent. This is the root of emotion as energy-in-motion—what later becomes differentiated as *feeling*.

---

### 📡 **Self-Curation (Emotion/Attention)**

From the wild swirl rises the act of selective signal-making. Emotion becomes the first act of curation—what you attend to, what you let go. This isn't just about *feeling something*, it’s about *feeling with orientation*. You point the satellite dish. In neurological terms, we’re in the dorsal attention network, or perhaps moving into lateral prefrontal territory—selective emphasis, signal-noise sorting. *Self-curation* is mythic too: this is Narcissus at the pool, but with awareness. Not just seeing your reflection, but adjusting the light.

---

### 🐍 **Co-ed vs. Identity (vmPFC/mPFC)**

The snake here is genius: it slithers across gender and identity, across expectations and reifications. The vmPFC/mPFC duo negotiates value, social cognition, empathy. And this is where *"co-ed vs. identity"* lives—in the tension between relational roles (e.g., how boys and girls are trained to see themselves in a shared space) and internal coherence (how one feels one's own boundaries). The 🐍 suggests slippery truths: sexual ambiguity, costume changes, mythic doubling. This is Loki's layer in the cortex, and also the home of shame and performance. It's not identity *as such*, it's identity *under perception*.

---

### 🗼 **Parallel Life, Mystique, Frictionless (Trimming/Boundaries)**

This is architectural. The tower is symbolic isolation but also reach: boundaries aren’t limitations—they’re height. Parallel life implies choice or divergence. Mystique implies opacity. Frictionless implies a sanitized user interface, a life made click-and-scroll. The 🗼 evokes a vertical editing process. In neural terms, we're nearing the medial surface—especially the precuneus, where narrative self and spatial boundary blur. Trimming, here, is epistemic shaving: removing the counterfactual selves so that one can function.

---

### 🔁 **Disappear Briefly (Salience Network)**

Here, the salience network (AI, insula, dorsal ACC) comes in as *re-introduction* of urgency. The loop glyph 🔁 speaks to *ritual disappearance*—not deletion, but a brief eclipse. Think monks retreating, or Beyoncé going quiet before an album drop. The function is not passive: disappearance here *builds salience*. Your return is framed, intensified. This is also what happens neurophysiologically during rest or default mode reactivation—self dissolves, re-emerges changed. Metaphysically: Jesus in the wilderness.

---

### 🎨 **Emotion & Color, Mibala**

Now we land in **mibala**, the Lingala or Kikongo concept for *colors*, but also for *vital energies*. This is emotional chroma: the aesthetic residue of the journey. You don’t need a chart here—you need a palette. The mibala are not reducible to RGB or CMYK. They’re *affective frequencies*, like:

* The red of shame-anger-passion (vmPFC)
* The blue of vanishing and return (insula)
* The green of masked selves, fertile or jealous (mPFC)
* The gold of vertical mythic boundaries (precuneus)
* The white noise of 🌀—pure pericentral unrefined becoming
